# MassLogic Branding &amp; Identity Standards (v1.1)

These guidelines define the correct usage of our marks to maintain brand authority and legal protection across the ecosystem.

### 🛡️ Core Marks
The following are trademarks of MassLogic. The trademark symbol (™) must be used on the first or most prominent mention of each mark on a page:

- **MIMO™ by MassLogic** (The Neutral AI Operating System)
- **MIMO Marketplace™** (The Expert Agent Hub)
- **Brand Brain™** (The Proprietary Intelligence Hub)
- **Marketing Operating Model™** (The Strategic Framework)
- **MassLogic™** (The Parent Brand)

### ✅ Style &amp; Usage Rules
- **The "First Mention" Rule:** Use the ™ symbol only on the first mention. Subsequent mentions in the same document should use the wordmark without the symbol.
- **MIMO:** Always all-caps. Never "Mimo" or "mimo."
- **MassLogic:** Always camel case with a capital **M** and **L**. Never "Masslogic."
- **Nomenclature:** Refer to the platform as "MIMO™ by MassLogic" to establish brand authority.

### 🏛️ Legal Entity &amp; Entity-Neutrality
To maintain a clean, product-led brand identity, we adhere to the following entity-neutrality standards:
- **No Entity Suffixes:** Do not include "LLC," "Inc.," or other legal suffixes in brand-facing materials, including logos, banners, headers, or marketing copy.
- **Brand vs. Entity:** Use "MassLogic" for all branding and product references. Reserved use of "MassLogic, LLC" is restricted solely to formal legal contracts and the mandatory attribution footer.

### 🚫 Incorrect Usage
- Do not abbreviate "MIMO" or "MassLogic."
- Do not combine these marks with other identifiers without express permission.
- Do not alter the MassLogic logo or secondary marks (no rotating, skewing, or color shifts).

### ⚖️ Legal Attribution
Include this standard attribution in the footer or credits of all materials:
&gt; "MIMO™, MIMO Marketplace™, Brand Brain™, Marketing Operating Model™, MassLogic™, and the MassLogic logo are trademarks of MassLogic. All other trademarks are the property of their respective owners."

# MassLogic Labs — Branding & Identity Standards (v3.0)

> Source of truth: the **MassLogic Labs Brand Hub** (Notion). If this file conflicts with the Hub, the Hub wins. This file supersedes BRANDING.md v1.1, which is retired.

These standards define correct usage of our names and marks. For the full visual system (color, typography, logo files, components), work from the Brand Hub and the Visual ID books in the Brand Canon.

## Naming & verbal canon

- **MassLogic Labs, Inc.** is the legal entity. Use the full legal name in footers and company lockups only. Never "MassLogic," "Masslogic," or "MassLogic LLC."
- **MIMO** is the master brand and the platform. All-caps, always, in written text. It leads everywhere and stands alone in headlines. Do not expand the acronym in short form. (Exception: the MIMO logo/wordmark is set lowercase — "mimo" — per the MIMO Visual ID. The logotype and written usage are different.)
- **MIMO is the platform** (the noun). Name a sub-brand only when describing that specific layer.
- Do **not** call MIMO "neutral." It is vendor-agnostic — say "governs every AI, regardless of vendor." ("Neutral" is Presciant's positioning and blurs the two businesses.)
- The MarTech stack manages branding. **MIMO governs brand.** Do not describe MIMO as a MarTech tool.
- Canonical domain: **masslogiclabs.com**. Do not use mass-logic.com or masslogic.io.

## Platform taxonomy

Name a layer only when describing that layer.

- **MIMO Workspace** — the CMO command surface; what a customer deploys and sees.
- **MIMO Intelligence** — the governed context and memory layer.
- **MIMO Orchestration** — agent and MarTech orchestration.
- **MIMO Marketspace** — the curated marketplace for licensed IP and certified agents.

**Pillars (what MIMO does):** Ground → Orchestrate → Surface (governance is the spine). Mapped to layers: Ground → MIMO Intelligence, Orchestrate → MIMO Orchestration, Surface → MIMO Workspace.

**Retired terms — do not use:** "Brand Brain" (now MIMO Intelligence), "Marketplace" (now Marketspace), "Marketing Operating Model" (not part of the canon), "MIMO by MassLogic," "Neutral AI Operating System," "MIMO Prime" (undefined/unbuilt concept — not canon).

## Incorrect usage

- Do not abbreviate or alter "MIMO" or "MassLogic Labs."
- Do not expand MIMO as competing acronyms on the same page (one register per document).
- Do not alter, recolor, stretch, or reconstruct any mark.
- Do not combine these marks with other identifiers without written permission.
